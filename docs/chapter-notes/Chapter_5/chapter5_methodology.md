# Chapter 5 Methodology

Business question: What prevents Canadians and Health donors from giving more?

The chapter uses official SGVP `NG_*` reasons-for-not-giving-more variables verified against the English codebook/SAS layout. Rates use 1=Yes over valid 1/2 yes/no denominators. Valid skip, don't know, refusal, and not stated are excluded from valid denominators. `NG_120*` efficiency follow-ups and `NG_140*` request follow-ups are kept separate from core barriers because their applicability is conditional.
